Important Information

What happens after you submit?

  • Escrow funds remain locked during the investigation period to protect both parties.
  • The seller will be notified immediately after your dispute claim is submitted.
  • The seller will have an opportunity to submit an official response with their own evidence.
  • AssetHub administrators will review all evidence from both sides before making a final decision.
  • You will receive notifications and/or emails when the dispute status changes.
