Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about how AssetHub's marketplace, escrow system, and dispute process work.

What is AssetHub?
AssetHub is a peer-to-peer marketplace prototype that allows users to buy and sell items securely using an escrow-based transaction system.
How does the escrow system work?
The buyer's payment is held until the seller delivers the item and the buyer confirms that it meets the agreed conditions. This helps increase trust between both parties.
Can buyers and sellers trade without using escrow?
Yes. AssetHub also supports direct transactions, allowing buyers and sellers to communicate and complete transactions outside the escrow process if they choose.
What happens if there is a disagreement?
Buyers can open a dispute, provide supporting evidence, and the administrator can review the case before deciding whether the payment should be released to the seller or refunded to the buyer.

Need urgent escrow assistance?

Our dispute resolution team is available 24/6 for high-value asset transactions.
